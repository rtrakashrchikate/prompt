This campaign is a white-hat, BCI-compliant, one-time SEO authority blitz designed to establish Advocate Akash Chikate as the dominant online authority for criminal defence in Pune, Maharashtra. The campaign covers:

- Local search domination (Google Business Profile + local citations)
- E-E-A-T authority building (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust)
- Outreach and digital PR
- Technical SEO and schema markup

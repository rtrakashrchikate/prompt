## Strict Campaign Rules

- No black-hat SEO (no PBNs, no paid spam links, no keyword stuffing)
- No fake reviews or manipulated ratings
- No fabricated credentials, cases, or achievements
- No guarantee language or outcome promises (BCI compliance)
- No comparative superlatives ("best lawyer in Pune" — BCI prohibited)
- No fee disclosure in public content
- All content must be informational/educational in positioning
- All BNSS/BNS/BPSS 2023 section references must be legally accurate
- All statistics must cite official sources (NCRB, NJDG, government reports)
- All case studies must be fully anonymised with educational framing
